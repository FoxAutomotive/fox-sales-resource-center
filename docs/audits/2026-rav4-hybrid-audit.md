# 2026 RAV4 Hybrid data-quality audit

Reviewed 2026-11-03. Source: supplied 30-page Toyota brochure, especially pp. 20–25. Its SHA-256 is `b3bf909f6220343c8548ee9b004935317679a8c5e1444537083821e3896075a1`.

## Results

**93 previously Unverified cells reviewed: 36 resolved, 57 retained as genuinely unestablished by the reviewed evidence.**

- **9 Standard:** the explicit 170 lb-ft (231 N·m) maximum-torque row on p. 25. The basis remains unspecified; it is not presented as combined-system or engine-only torque.
- **27 Unavailable / not applicable:** plug-in electric range, plug-in charging and DC charging for nine conventional HEV configurations. This is a powertrain-class applicability correction, not a claim that the HEV has no short-distance EV Mode.
- **57 still Unverified:** 8 fuel-economy ratings, 3 FWD towing ratings, and 46 equipment blanks whose negative meaning is not defined in this brochure.

The existing Standard and Optional marks for moonroofs, seats, steering wheels, power liftgates, Qi charging and driver assistance were also rechecked. No supported positive S/A mark was left Unverified. Source pages are retained per cell. The XSE moonroof value now describes only its Standard moonroof; the panoramic alternative remains Optional in the separate listing.

## Why blanks are not automatically Unavailable

The supplied legend defines S and A but does not define blank as “not offered.” Moreover, p. 18 explicitly names the XSE JBL package while the nine-speaker JBL row on p. 24 has no XSE availability mark. A blank cannot safely be converted into a categorical factory exclusion without confirming the package/ordering information. The audit does not infer features from another trim or an earlier model year.

## Technical research limits

The Toyota media-kit PDF and EPA lookup both failed at proxy CONNECT with HTTP 403. Neither source was read, so no EPA-certified MPG values or missing FWD towing limits are invented. The blocked URLs and outcomes are recorded in the machine-readable ledger. Enabling those destinations or supplying the official records is the next step for those technical cells.

## Other remaining limitations

The brochure lists eight HEV package names but not their itemized contents or every combination/region/drivetrain restriction. Those remain explicitly unverified. Total seating capacity remains unconfirmed in the reviewed sources even though the documented split-fold rear-seat feature is Standard. PHEV information, other models, branding, layout and comparison behavior are untouched.

## Reproducibility

[Machine-readable cell ledger](2026-rav4-hybrid-audit.json) records each original unknown, final state, page review and rationale. Tests reconcile every entry against the live catalog, protect the remaining unknowns, check the S/A availability patterns and prevent unread EPA sources from becoming evidence. Commit-scope checks and historical SHA-256 snapshots confirm that this audit preserved other models, PHEV records and existing package listings; those snapshots are not permanent restrictions on future legitimate research updates.
