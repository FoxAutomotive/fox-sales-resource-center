## What's new — reviewed comparisons

### Atlas

- Volkswagen identifies the 2027 Atlas as ALL NEW. Output changes from 269 hp / 273 lb-ft to 282 hp / 258 lb-ft; the higher horsepower is accompanied by lower quoted torque. (2026 pp. 6; 2027 pp. 5)
- The listed lineup is now SE, SE w/ Technology, SEL R-Line® and SEL Premium R-Line®. Atlas Peak Edition and the plain SEL name from 2026 are not listed in this edition. (2026 pp. 2; 2027 pp. 2)
- The standard 12-inch center display is replaced by a 12.9-inch display on SE and a 15-inch display on SE w/ Technology and above. Dual front Qi chargers replace the single front charger. (2026 pp. 8; 2027 pp. 7)
- SEL R-Line® and SEL Premium R-Line® add active ventilation for outboard second-row seats; 2026 documented rear heating without ventilation. New Varenna/Nappa upholstery choices, real wood décor and revised ambient lighting are detailed by trim. (2026 pp. 7; 2027 pp. 6)
- Front/rear Park Distance Control and hands-free Easy Open/Easy Close are now standard even on SE; both were unavailable on 2026 SE. (2026 pp. 8; 2027 pp. 7)
- Travel Assist adds assisted lane changing and Emergency Assist becomes Emergency Assist Plus. SEL Premium R-Line® adds Park Assist Plus with Memory Parking; the new seven-airbag protection system is documented across trims. (2026 pp. 8; 2027 pp. 7)
- The SE w/ Technology Black Wheel Package changes from 20-inch 40T wheels to 54U wheels; the 2027 package carries limited availability ending CW02/27. (2026 pp. 2; 2027 pp. 2)

Open questions: Volkswagen labels the model ALL NEW; the order guide does not establish new exterior/interior measurements or fuel economy. The p. 5 photo caption still mentions Atlas Peak Edition, although that trim is absent from the configuration and equipment tables. Confirm any later trim release separately.

### Atlas Cross Sport

- Volkswagen identifies the 2027 Atlas Cross Sport as ALL NEW. Output changes from 269 hp / 273 lb-ft to 282 hp / 258 lb-ft. (2026 pp. 13; 2027 pp. 10)
- SE and SE w/ Technology retain FWD/AWD listings, followed by AWD SEL R-Line® and SEL Premium R-Line®. The 2026 plain SEL and SEL R-Line® Black names are no longer listed. (2026 pp. 9; 2027 pp. 8)
- The 12-inch center display is replaced by 12.9 inches on SE and 15 inches on higher trims. Dual front Qi chargers replace the single front charger. (2026 pp. 15; 2027 pp. 12)
- SEL R-Line® and SEL Premium R-Line® now document heated and actively ventilated outboard rear seats, plus Varenna/Nappa upholstery and revised interior lighting. The 2026 table documented rear heating without ventilation. (2026 pp. 14; 2027 pp. 11)
- SE gains standard front/rear Park Distance Control and hands-free Easy Open/Easy Close, previously unavailable on SE. Assisted lane changing, Emergency Assist Plus and a seven-airbag system are documented; SEL Premium R-Line® adds Memory Parking. (2026 pp. 15; 2027 pp. 12)

Open questions: All retail, destination, paint and package prices remain TBA in this edition. New measurements, fuel economy and detailed redesign specifications need the 2027 technical guide.

### Tiguan

- SE R-Line® Black gains a standard heated steering wheel; the 2026 equipment matrix marked it not offered on that trim. The 2027 production credit 2PD can remove steering-wheel heating, so verify the actual window sticker. (2026 pp. 21; 2027 pp. 15, 17)
- The driver-assistance table now names Emergency Assist Plus instead of Emergency Assist. (2026 pp. 22; 2027 pp. 18)

Open questions: The PJ2 wheel package still has conflicting wheel codes: U24 on p. 13 versus 48H on p. 16. Confirm the correct code with Volkswagen. Detailed 2027 fuel economy and measurements are not supplied; 2026 technical values have not been copied.

### Taos

- A new Sport trim is listed in FWD and 4MOTION AWD, between S and SE. Its equipment includes GOAL-pattern cloth with yellow contrast stitching, black exterior accents and drivetrain-specific black wheels. (2026 pp. 23, 26; 2027 pp. 19, 23)
- S gains standard MIB3 Composition Media and a six-speaker sound system. The 2026 S listing used Composition Color and did not offer the six-speaker system. (2026 pp. 27; 2027 pp. 24)

Open questions: The SE FWD P35 wheel option from 2026 is not listed in the 2027 factory option table; absence from this non-exhaustive guide is not confirmation of discontinuation. Technical measurements, fuel economy and a tow rating require additional documentation.

### Golf GTI

- Travel Assist now documents assisted lane changing across S, SE and Autobahn; the 2026 table described Travel Assist without assisted lane changing. (2026 pp. 36; 2027 pp. 28)
- Emergency Assist Plus replaces the previously named Emergency Assist in the standard IQ.DRIVE equipment listing. (2026 pp. 36; 2027 pp. 28)

Open questions: No other major equipment redesign or powertrain change is established by these two editions. 2027 EPA economy, measurements and any broader launch changes need technical/release documentation.

### Golf R

- Golf R Black Edition is newly listed beside Golf R. Its documented standard distinctions include gloss-black 19-inch wheels, black badging/logos and black exhaust tips. (2026 pp. 37, 39; 2027 pp. 29, 31)
- Akrapovič titanium exhaust is now a standalone factory option 0P8 for either trim. The 2026 guide bundled it in Euro Style Package POA. The POA roof/ventilation deletion package is not listed in this edition; do not label the retained standard roof and ventilated seats as newly added equipment. (2026 pp. 37, 39; 2027 pp. 29, 31)
- Travel Assist adds assisted lane changing and the standard suite names Emergency Assist Plus instead of Emergency Assist. (2026 pp. 40; 2027 pp. 32)

Open questions: The guide does not expressly confirm discontinuation of Euro Style Package POA; its absence is a listing change. Detailed 2027 fuel economy and technical measurements remain unresolved.

### Jetta 1.5T

- Push-button engine start is now standard on S and Sport as well as SE and SEL; the 2026 table marked S and Sport not offered. (2026 pp. 45; 2027 pp. 37)
- S and Sport now receive MIB3 Composition Media instead of Composition Color. SE and SEL retain their documented systems rather than being labeled new. (2026 pp. 45; 2027 pp. 37)
- The 2027 guide explicitly marks the front/rear seatbelt reminder system NEW and standard on every trim; it was not listed in the reviewed 2026 equipment table. (2026 pp. 45; 2027 pp. 37)

Open questions: Unlisted 2026 seatbelt-reminder equipment is not treated as proven absence; this change follows the explicit 2027 NEW label. Technical measurements and 2027 fuel economy require additional documentation. The p. 35 JDD Dynamic Center Caps retail amount is printed as $22, while comparable entries elsewhere in the guide are $228. The $22 source value is retained; manufacturer confirmation is needed.

### Jetta GLI

- The 2027 configuration table lists Autobahn with seven-speed DSG only; the 2026 table listed both six-speed manual and DSG. Do not carry the manual configuration into 2027 from the previous year. (2026 pp. 46; 2027 pp. 38)
- The front/rear seatbelt reminder system is explicitly marked NEW and standard in the 2027 guide. No 2026 absence is inferred from its omission in that table. (2026 pp. 49; 2027 pp. 41)

Open questions: The p. 38 illustration caption still says manual although the priced table lists only DSG. Manual availability requires Volkswagen clarification; the caption is not used to invent a configuration. The 2026 projector-headlamp description is absent from the 2027 table, so removal is not claimed. Fuel economy and technical measurements require the 2027 specifications.
